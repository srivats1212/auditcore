## What changed to get here

- **Scope by product area.** Before October 2024 most domestic drug and
  device letters were issued by ORA field divisions, not by CDER or CDRH.
  Filtering on center names would have dropped about 150 of the 656 letters.
- **483 Response records skipped.** The reading room lists the firm's reply
  next to each 483; it quotes every observation and would have been read as
  FDA's findings.
- **Segmentation.** 60 letters with one unnumbered violation, 21 483s with
  inline headings ("WE OBSERVED: Observation 1"), and amended 483s that
  repeat observation numbers now segment. Two letters and 47 text 483s still
  produce no observations.
- **OCR flag.** Scanned 483s often carry text only on FDA's boilerplate back
  page; the flag now catches them (327, up from 163).

## LLM run

violation text with the proposals copied to sibling citations, paper assumed
unless an electronic record is stated.
